# Mean reversion (version 1)

Long only. Looks for a sharp dip below the lower Bollinger band inside a long-term uptrend, entered on the first up day.

Checks (points): close above the 200-day average (25); close below the lower band (20, 2) on one of the last 2 days (25); RSI(14) below 30 on one of the last 3 days (20); ADX(14) below 25, not a strong trend (15); first up day, close above the previous close (15).

BUY when the uptrend, band and up-day checks pass and the 20-day average is above the entry. Target: the 20-day average. Stop: 1.5 ATR below entry. Time exit: 10 trading days. A dip can keep falling: news-driven drops (earnings, guidance) are the main risk.
