# Volume accumulation (version 1)

Long only. Looks for steady buying pressure under a rising price, on completed daily bars. Separate from the volume-surge alert.

Checks (points): OBV trend over 20 days above 0.2, more volume on up days than down days (25); Chaikin money flow (20) above 0.05 (20); money flow index (14) between 50 and 80 (15); an up close on at least 1.5x average volume in the last 3 days (20); close above the 50-day average (20).

BUY when the OBV, money flow and 50-day checks pass. Stop: 1.5 ATR below entry. Target: twice the risk. Invalidation: a close below the stop. Time exit: 20 trading days.

Judge the trade on whether the buying is steady or one block trade, and on earnings and news.
