# Price action pullback (version 1)

Long only. Looks for a pullback to support inside an uptrend that ends with a bullish candle, on completed daily bars. A candle alone never makes a BUY.

Checks (points): swing structure up (25); in the last 3 days the low came within 1 ATR of the nearest support or the 20-day EMA (25); a bullish engulfing, hammer or morning star on the last day (20); close above the most recent swing low (15); nearest resistance at least twice the risk away, or none above (15).

BUY when the structure, support and candle checks pass. Stop: the pullback low minus a quarter ATR. Target: twice the risk. Invalidation: a close below the stop. Time exit: 20 trading days.

Judge the trade on whether the uptrend is intact and the support is real, and on what is overhead.
